Here's the official (unofficial) roadmap: a few trips where we get hopelessly lost together, the slightly terrifying but very necessary conversation with our parents, me learning to gently talk your overthinking brain down from whatever ledge it's climbed onto this time, and both of us figuring out how to keep the house standing on days the love overflows a little too enthusiastically and floods the kitchen.
